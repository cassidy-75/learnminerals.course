Do I need capital to do a deal? No. Module 6 shows you how to assign the deal to a buyer and collect, instead of funding it yourself.
How long does it take? About eight hours of video. Most students finish inside two weeks, working their first county while they watch.
I've never worked in oil and gas. Is this too advanced? No. It starts at zero. Most students had never opened a county record before Module 2.
Does this only work in Texas? No. The process is the same in any producing state. Students are working Texas, Oklahoma, New Mexico, Colorado, and North Dakota.
Can I do this part time? Yes. Records are online and owners take calls in the evening. Most students start around a full-time job.
How fast could a first deal happen? That’s up to the work you put in and your market. Some students have a deal working inside a few weeks, others take longer. Nothing here is a guarantee.
What is the onboarding call? Right after checkout you’ll book a short setup call to activate your account, load your tools, and point you at the right module for your state. Free if you book inside your first 7 days.
Is this coaching? No. This is the self-paced course: videos, tools, and templates you work through yourself. The only call is a short onboarding call to activate your account.
